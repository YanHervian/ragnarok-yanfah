## Visual Consistency Rules

- Maintain Valkren’s core palette: black, white, gunmetal, and crimson
red.
- Crimson red is the primary energy/VFX color.
- White is reserved for intense energy cores, flashes, and hot impact
highlights.
- Do not introduce gold, green, blue, purple, or other dominant
colors.
- Keep the VFX clean and readable at game resolution.
- Use crisp pixel-art edges and deliberate pixel clusters.
- Avoid photorealistic effects, soft 3D rendering, realistic smoke, or
painterly textures.
- Do not include characters, text, UI, logos, or scenery unless
explicitly requested.
- All effects must work on a solid black background for
screen/additive blending.
- The overall visual language should feel like a premium anime battle
game featuring a heavy futuristic mecha.
