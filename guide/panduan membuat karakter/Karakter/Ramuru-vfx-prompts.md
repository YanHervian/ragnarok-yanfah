## Visual Consistency Rules

- Maintain Ramuru's core VFX palette: bright cyan, teal, white, and darker blue for contrast.
- The effects should feel like fluid water and viscous slime, completely different from Valkren's mechanical fire/sparks.
- Incorporate subtle, occasional tech/glitch squares to match the techwear aesthetic without losing the organic water feel.
- Do not introduce red, green, or purple.
- Keep the VFX clean and readable at game resolution.
- Use crisp pixel-art edges and deliberate pixel clusters.
- All effects must work on a solid black background for screen/additive blending.
- No characters, weapons, or UI should be visible in the VFX generations.
