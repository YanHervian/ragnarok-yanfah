# Dhyla - Sprite Animation Prompts
## Character Reference (pasang di setiap prompt)
Dhyla is a beautiful divine goddess warrior. Dark curly hair. Wearing an elegant sleeveless white dress with gold trim and a thigh slit. Gold shoulder armor pads. Gold bracelets and ankle bands. Completely barefoot. She wears a large, wide, elegant white mushroom cap hat with gold patterns on top of her head. Anime pixel art style. Divine faction. Do NOT add any magical effects, sparks, glows, or particles unless specifically stated.

---
## Canvas Rules
- **4-frame animations**: Canvas 1792x512 px (4 equal frames of 448x512 px each). Solid magenta (#FF00FF) background.
- **1-frame animations** (jump, doublejump): Canvas 448x512 px (single frame). Solid magenta (#FF00FF) background.

Generate a 4-frame horizontal sprite strip for Dhyla.

Canvas: 1792x512 px (4 frames of 448x512 px). Magenta (#FF00FF) background.

Frame 4 (Exhale): Shoulders drop as she exhales. Her head returns to center, slight downward tilt. Dress swings back to center. Everything returning to Frame 1, ready to loop.
